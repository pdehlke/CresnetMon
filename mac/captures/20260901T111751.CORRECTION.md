# Correction to 20260901T111751.jsonl

Both bursts in that capture carry `"device": {"id": "0x6F", "model": "CNX-B8",
"room": "Kitchen"}`. That label is wrong. The Living Pathway button was pressed
on **0x6A, the Great Room keypad**, confirmed by the owner 2026-09-01.

The program descriptor agrees on the identities:

    Slot-01.ID-6A;8;;CNX-B8;105-Great Room;CNX-B8;-11
    Slot-01.ID-6F;10;;CNX-B8;101-Kitchen;CNX-B8;-11

The frames are unaffected. They are addressed to the CLX dimmer modules and
carry no source address, so which keypad originated the press changes nothing
about the bytes. The JSONL is left as recorded rather than edited, so that the
capture stays a faithful record of what the tool wrote; this note is the
correction.
